## Project-specific expectations

JEREBU.EXE deals with a cross-border subject in which nationality, politics and blame can run high. In addition to the Covenant, these expectations apply.

1. **No hostility or stereotyping based on nationality, ethnicity, religion or language.** This includes people in Indonesia, Malaysia and Singapore, and anyone else. People in Indonesia are also affected by fires and haze. Criticise arguments, sources and methods, never peoples.

2. **No unverified accusations against identifiable people, companies or officials.** Do not claim that a named person, company, official or government caused a fire or haze event without sufficient reliable evidence. A satellite hotspot on its own proves nothing about who is responsible. An allegation that someone else has made must be labelled as an allegation and cited. Unsupported accusations will be removed. This applies everywhere: issues, pull requests, reviews, commit messages, code comments and documentation.

3. **No doxxing, and no sharing of personal data.** Do not publish anyone's home address, phone number, identity documents, health information, private messages or other personal details. This includes people you believe have done wrong. Do not post confidential or leaked documents.

4. **Good-faith disagreement about evidence is welcome.** Challenging a claim, a source, a method or an experiment is a contribution, not an attack. Show your evidence, state your uncertainty, and assume good faith in others.

5. **AI-generated contributions meet the same standards.** The person who submits AI output is responsible for it, and must disclose AI use as the pull request template asks. Bulk-generated, unchecked or misleading content is not welcome, whoever or whatever produced it.

6. **Keep the focus on the project.** JEREBU.EXE is not an anti-Indonesia or anti-government campaign. Its spaces must not be used to run one, or to campaign against any country or its people.

## Reporting

Email **[JEREBU.EXE@proton.me](mailto:JEREBU.EXE@proton.me)**. You do not need a GitHub account. If you can, include:

- what happened, and when
- where it happened, with links if possible
- who was involved
- anything else that would help us understand the situation

Please share only the personal information needed to deal with the report. Reports are handled as confidentially as possible. The identity of the person reporting will not be shared without their permission.

## Who enforces this, honestly

The project currently has a **very small maintainer team: in practice, its founder alone**. Enforcement is carried out by the maintainers, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines. Responses are best effort, and there is no guaranteed response time.

AI tools may help a maintainer organise or summarise a report. Decisions about enforcement are always made by a human.

## Conflicts of interest

- A maintainer who is the subject of a report, or who has a close personal, professional or financial connection to someone involved, will not handle that report.
- Because there is currently only one maintainer, that is not always possible. If your report concerns the founder, or you believe the founder has a conflict of interest, say so in your report. The founder will acknowledge the conflict and, where possible, ask an independent person to review the report. You can also use GitHub's own tools for reporting content or abuse.
- Contributors and maintainers with a material interest in a topic should disclose it in the relevant issue or pull request. Examples include working for, or holding a financial interest in, an organisation being discussed. Disclosure does not exclude anyone from taking part. It lets others weigh the contribution fairly.
